About Southport Key Club

Building leaders through service since 2001

Our Story

Southport Key Club was founded with a simple mission: to empower students to become leaders through service to their community. Over the past years, we've grown from a small group of dedicated students to one of the largest and most active Key Clubs in Indiana.

Our members participate in a wide range of service activities, from local food banks, churches, community clean-ups, to any local events. We believe that every act of service, no matter how small, has the power to create positive change.

Through our partnership with Kiwanis International, we provide students with opportunities to develop leadership skills, build lasting friendships, and make a real difference in the world around them.
