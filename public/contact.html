Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join Key Club?

You can join by filling out our registration form before September 4th. Check our home page for the link to the Google Form.

What are the requirements?

Any Southport High School student can join. We ask for a commitment of at least 9 service hours per semester.

When do you meet?

We meet every two fridays from 12:55 PM to 1:55 PM in the Auditorium. You must attend atleast 4 of these meetings per semester in order to meet requirements.

What kind of service activities do you do?

We participate in food drives, help out at food banks, community clean-ups, church volunteering events, holiday events, and various fundraisers throughout the school year.

Is there a membership fee?

Yes, there is an annual fee of $20 that covers Kiwanis International dues. Financial assistance is available, but you must reach out to us.

Can I still join if I'm involved in other activities?

Absolutely! Many of our members are involved in sports, band, and other clubs. We offer flexible scheduling for service opportunities.
