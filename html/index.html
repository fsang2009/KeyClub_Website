Our Mission

At Key Club, our mission is to build leadership through serving others. Members learn to lead by doing good work in their local schools and communities. We care for our neighbors and make the world a better place every single day. Together, we grow into strong and caring leaders for the future.

Our Pledge

I pledge, on my honor, to uphold the objects of Key Club International; to build my home, school and community; to serve my nation and God; and combat all forces which tend to undermine these institutions.
